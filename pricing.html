Questions creators ask

Do I have to pay to be on a list?

No. Editors choose creators for each list from their public data, and that's free. Paid plans only add a separate, labelled featured spot.

Will paying move me up the numbered list?

No. The numbered list is editorial. Your featured spot appears in addition to it, marked as paid.

Which lists can I appear on?

Lists that match your niche and location, for example "Best Gaming Influencers in India". If the right list doesn't exist yet, tell us and we'll consider building it.

Where does my data come from?

Your follower count, engagement and recent posts come from Instagram's official API and refresh automatically. Any creator can claim their profile for free to correct their location, niche and contact email.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. Your spot stays live until the end of the period you've paid for.

What if a list's featured spots are full?

You can join the waitlist for that list, or choose another list that fits. Yearly plans get first pick of open spots.

Not sure which plan fits?

Start with Featured. You can switch plans anytime.
